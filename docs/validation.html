Checks Already Built In

  • Spectra are normalized after generation and after each convolution.
  • The endpoint is cut off at E > Q for zero or positive mass.
  • Pileup uses zero-padded convolution through SciPy, avoiding circular wraparound.
  • Progress can be saved and reloaded without changing accumulated counts.

Recommended Next Checks

  • Scan live time and confirm approximate m_nu proportional t^-1/4 behavior.
  • Compare 1.0 eV HOLMES-like and 0.2 eV KID-like resolution inputs.
  • Scan activity_bq and tau_eff_us to see when pileup dominates.
  • Add a slower maximum-likelihood pseudo-experiment fitter for comparison.

Caveats

This is a trade-study simulator. The atomic line model is simplified and omits shake-up, shake-off, exchange, overlap corrections, gain drift, nonlinearity, and per-pixel calibration effects. Final sensitivity claims need a more complete nuisance-parameter fit and validation against published HOLMES/Nucciotti calculations.
