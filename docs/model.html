Single-Event Spectrum

The detector measures calorimetric energy E_c. The simplified spectrum is 

S(E_c) = phase_space(E_c, Q, mnu2) * sum_i P_i L(E_c; E_i, Gamma_i)

The phase-space factor is (Q - E_c) sqrt((Q - E_c)^2 - mnu2), clipped to zero above the physical endpoint. Negative mnu2 is allowed only internally for stable finite-difference derivatives.

Detector Response

Energy resolution is a Gaussian convolution with sigma = FWHM / 2.355. The convolution is normalized after smoothing to avoid numerical drift.

Pileup

The unresolved pileup density is the self-convolution of the single-event spectrum. Its fraction is 

f_pp = activity_bq_per_pixel * tau_eff_seconds

Activity is per detector pixel, not the full array rate. The GUI reports the resulting f_pp for the current inputs. It plots the normalized self-convolution as Normalized pileup curve and the accepted contribution f_pp * pileup_density as Pileup passing filter.

Counts

The simulator does not draw individual Ho decays. For each chunk, it computes binned expected counts and draws one Poisson value per bin. The fit-window bins keep their absolute probability, so only the fraction of events landing in the selected energy range contributes to the histogram. This is the right scale for exposures with 10^14 to 10^15 physical events.

Fit Uncertainty Projection

The floated fit directions include mnu2, normalization, and optionally flat background. The Q-value is fixed in the GUI estimator because a robust Q-floating fit is more expensive and should be added after this first trade-study tool is validated.

The sensitivity history also reports Monte Carlo fit variation. For each exposure point it draws the configured number of independent cumulative Poisson histograms, fits them concurrently, and stores the mean and standard deviation of the signed fitted neutrino-mass scale. It also stores the RMS error of the physical fitted mass relative to the injected mass. The GUI fits the newer half of those RMS-error values to A / sqrt(t) and reports where that curve reaches half of the injected mass.
